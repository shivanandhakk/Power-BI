# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard | Microsoft Power BI

An interactive, three-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance across **2017–2019**, transforming transactional data into meaningful business insights through KPIs, visualizations, and interactive filtering.

# 📌 *Project Overview* 

This project explores business performance across sales, profitability, products, customers, geographical regions, and salespeople.  
It combines data preparation, data modeling, and DAX calculations to present key metrics in a clear, interactive report.

# 📑 *Dashboard Pages*
 📈 **Sales Performance** | Sales trends, order patterns, geographical sales, and salesperson contribution.   
 🛍️ **Product Performance & Profitability** | Product reach, cost versus profit, and margin analysis.   
 🎯 **Business Performance Overview** | Overall profit, profit margin, customer metrics, and yearly sales summary. 

# 📁 *Project Files*
 📊 `Sales_Report_2017_18_19.pbix` | **Interactive Power BI report** containing the data model, DAX measures, three dashboard pages, slicers, and page navigation. Open with Power BI Desktop to explore the report.   
 📄 `Sales_Report_2017_18_19.pdf` | **PDF report export** providing a static view of the dashboard pages for easy viewing and sharing.  
 📗 `sales_dataset.xlsx` | **Source dataset** containing sales transactions from 2017–2019 and supporting product, location, customer, and salesperson data. 

# 🛠️ *Tools & Technologies*

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **DAX** — Measures for sales, cost, profit, margins, averages, and month-over-month growth.
- **Power Query** — Data loading and transformation.
- **Data Modeling** — Star-schema model connecting sales data with customer, product, location, salesperson, and date tables.
- **Microsoft Excel** — Source data management

# 🔍 *Key Analysis Areas*

- 📈 **Sales Trends:** Monthly sales and month-over-month growth
- 💰 **Profitability:** Quarterly profit, profit margins, revenue, and cost analysis
- 🛍️ **Product Insights:** Customer reach, product rankings, cost versus profit, and low-margin products
- 👥 **Customer Analysis:** Customer trends, sales per customer, and orders per customer
- 🌍 **Geographical Performance:** Top counties by sales
- 🧑‍💼 **Salesperson Analysis:** Profit contribution and sales per salesperson
- 🎯 **Business KPIs:** Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, Total Cost, Total Orders, Total Customers, Items Sold, Average Order Value, Average Selling Price, Profit per Order, Orders per Customer, Distinct Products Sold, Sales per Customer, Sales per Salesperson, and Active Salespeople
- 🎛️ **Interactive Exploration:** Date-range and location slicers for filtering report data

# 🎯 *Project Objective*

To demonstrate an end-to-end business intelligence workflow, from data preparation and modeling to DAX calculations and interactive dashboard design. The report brings key business metrics together to help stakeholders monitor performance, explore trends, and evaluate sales and profitability across multiple dimensions.
